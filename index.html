I am a professor and researcher at Universidad de Ingeniería y Tecnología (UTEC) in Lima, Peru, where I teach Introduction to Programming, Machine Learning, Computational Data Analysis and Capstone Project courses, and advise master’s theses in Data Science and Computer Science.

I received my B.Eng. in Computer Engineering from Universidad Católica San Pablo (UCSP, Arequipa, Peru), my M.Sc. in Computer Science from Universidade Federal de Ouro Preto (UFOP, Brazil), and my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais (UFMG, Brazil). In 2024 I was a postdoctoral researcher in the project V2IP: Video Surveillance for People and Vehicle Identification (UFPR–UFMG).

My research focuses on computer vision and machine learning, in particular:

  • anomalous event detection and surveillance video analysis,
  • person, face and vehicle detection and recognition (re-identification, open-set face recognition),
  • moving object detection and classification,
  • efficient deep models and attention mechanisms,
  • medical image analysis and data analysis.

From 2012–2014 and 2018–2022 I was a professor at UCSP, where I also coordinated the Computer Vision diploma program, the graduate program of the Computer Science Department, and the ACM Student Chapter. With my team, we won first place in the face recognition task of the 3rd Open-set Face Detection and Identification Challenge (IJCB 2024).

See my publications and teaching pages for more details.
